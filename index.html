About the App

Decision Maker is a simple utility that helps you make everyday choices — what to eat for lunch or dinner, yes or no, coin flip, dice roll, or your own custom options. Just tap Draw and let the app decide for you!

FAQ

  • How do I add custom options?
    Switch to the "Custom" tab, then tap "+ Add Option" to add your own choices.
  • Can I edit or delete an option?
    Tap an option to edit it, or swipe left to delete it.
  • How does the draw work?
    The app randomly selects one option from the current list with equal probability.
  • Can I shake to draw?
    Yes! Shake your device to trigger a draw at any time.
  • Where can I see past results?
    Tap "History" in the top right corner to view your recent draws.
