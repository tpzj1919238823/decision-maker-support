Privacy Policy

Decision Maker · Effective date: May 22, 2026

Introduction

Decision Maker ("we", "our", "the App") is a utility application that helps users make everyday decisions through random selection. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Custom options you create
  • Draw history (up to 20 recent records)

This data is saved using on-device storage (UserDefaults) and is never uploaded to any server.

Advertising

The App may display ads provided by Pangle (a third-party advertising platform). When advertising is enabled:

  • The App may request access to the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) via the App Tracking Transparency framework. You can choose to allow or deny this request.
  • The advertising SDK may collect device information (device model, OS version, screen size) and ad interaction data to serve relevant ads.

For details on Pangle's data practices, please refer to their privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party SDK:

  • Pangle Ad SDK — for displaying banner, open app, and rewarded video advertisements.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third party. The only external data exchange occurs through the advertising SDK as described above.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
