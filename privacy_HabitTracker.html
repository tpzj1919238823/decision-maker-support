Privacy Policy

Habit Tracker · Effective date: June 11, 2026

Introduction

Habit Tracker ("we", "our", "the App") is a productivity application that helps users build and maintain positive daily habits. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Habit names and emojis you create
  • Check-in dates and completion history
  • Streak counts and statistics

This data is saved using on-device storage (UserDefaults) and is never uploaded to any server.

Advertising

The App may display ads provided by Pangle and Google AdMob (third-party advertising platforms). When advertising is enabled:

  • The App may request access to the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) via the App Tracking Transparency framework. You can choose to allow or deny this request.
  • The advertising SDKs may collect device information (device model, OS version, screen size) and ad interaction data to serve relevant ads.

For details on each platform's data practices, please refer to their privacy policies linked below.
